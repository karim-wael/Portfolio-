# NASA Space Apps 2025: Weather Risk Modeling with ProbCast

Participating in the **October 4–5, 2025 NASA International Space Apps Challenge** demanded rapid engineering decisions and coordinated team management. As the Technical Team Lead for Team *Moon Walkers*, my goal was to transform satellite-derived Earth telemetry datasets into actionable environmental alerts for event planners—earning official **Galactic Problem Solver** project completion recognition (followed later in November 2026 by a second **Galactic Problem Solver** recognition with Team *The Fireflies* on **FLARE AI**).

## Data Pipelines & Telemetry

The platform, **ProbCast**, queries geographic coordinates using the **NASA POWER API** (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources). We designed a processing pipeline in Python to digest historical meteorology datasets:

- **Telemetry Ingestion**: Real-time querying fetches wind speed, surface humidity, precipitation levels, and solar radiation coordinates.
- **Statistical Modeling**: We implemented simple probabilistic rainfall thresholds using scientific python models.
- **Streamlit Frontend**: Rendered maps (using Folium) and interactive widgets enabled event organizers to immediately audit hazard exposure scores.

## Technical Leadership Under Pressure

In a hackathon setting, prioritizing delivery speed over architectural over-engineering is key. By managing workflow tasks between our data parsing team and frontend designers, we deployed a completely operational dashboard to public cloud endpoints within 48 hours, highlighting the value of focused, agile technical leadership.
